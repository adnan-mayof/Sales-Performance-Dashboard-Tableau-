## 1. **How do I use it?**

The dashboard is interactive and allows you to filter the data.

**Available Filter:**

* **Country**
<img width="1010" height="240" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/2025642d-c359-4328-8171-23b0c0794af9" />

When you select a country, the dashboard updates to show:

* **Business Summary** → Total sales amount, boxes shipped, and shipment count for that country
* **Sales by Country** → Focus only on the selected region
* **Top 10 Salespersons** → Highlight the top contributors in that specific country

All visualizations update automatically when the filter changes.

## 2. **Why does it matter?**

Sales data is critical for understanding **business growth, market opportunities, and team performance**. This dashboard helps:

* Monitor sales distribution by country
* Identify top-performing regions and individuals
* Guide decision-making with clear, visual insights

## 5. **What does it show?**

The dashboard provides three core insights:

1. **Business Summary** — $6.18M in sales, 177K boxes shipped, and 1,094 shipments overall.
2. **Sales by Country** — Visual comparison of sales volumes across regions like Australia, UK, India, USA, Canada, and New Zealand.
3. **Top 10 Salespersons** — Identifies leading contributors, either globally or within the filtered country.
